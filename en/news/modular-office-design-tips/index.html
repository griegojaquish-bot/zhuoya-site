Modular Office Design: How Should Light, Insulation and Layout Work Together?

A modular office does not have to look or feel generic. Orientation, windows, insulation and module combinations can make temporary or long-term workspaces more comfortable.

CONTENTS01　Plan daylight and orientation02　Coordinate insulation and ventilation03　Make the combination expandable

Plan daylight and orientation

Office areas benefit from stable natural light. Canopies, shading and well-positioned windows can reduce afternoon glare and excessive solar heat.

Coordinate insulation and ventilation

Roof and wall insulation, airtight details and air conditioning should reflect the local climate. Areas with higher occupancy also need suitable ventilation or fresh-air provision.

Make the combination expandable

Meeting rooms, open offices, management rooms and break areas can be arranged by frequency of use. This improves usable area without adding unnecessary circulation space.
