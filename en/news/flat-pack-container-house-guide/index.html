What Is a Flat-Pack Container House and Where Is It Used?

A flat-pack container house is based on standardised components. Most production can be completed in the factory before the system is transported and rapidly installed on site.

CONTENTS01　What does a flat-pack house include?02　What projects suit it?03　What information is needed before selection?

What does a flat-pack house include?

A typical system includes top and bottom frames, corner posts, wall panels, doors, windows and electrical fittings. Different configurations can serve offices, accommodation, welfare, storage and reception uses.

What projects suit it?

Construction site offices, worker accommodation, temporary offices, camp facilities, park expansion and emergency spaces are common applications.

What information is needed before selection?

Confirm the available area, expected occupancy, service period, floor arrangement, transport route and water and power conditions so the modules can be combined into a more suitable plan.

Need a project-specific recommendation?

Tell us your location, occupancy, schedule and intended use. The Zhuoya team can suggest a suitable product and space solution.
