How Can a Temporary Site Office Stay Cost-Effective and Useful?

Controlling the cost of a temporary office does not mean choosing the lowest specification. A sensible layout and reusable design can reduce the total project cost more effectively.

CONTENTS01　Build the core functions first02　Choose reusable combinations03　Include the supporting costs early

Build the core functions first

Prioritise offices, meetings, document storage, welfare and basic reception. Add expandable modules as the project team grows instead of overbuilding from the start.

Choose reusable combinations

Standard dimensions, demountable connections and common door and window configurations make future relocation, adaptation and reuse easier.

Include the supporting costs early

Foundations, water, power, air conditioning, network, transport and installation should all be included in the overall budget. Comparing only the unit price can hide the real cost.
