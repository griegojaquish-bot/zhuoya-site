What Should Be Prepared Before Exporting Modular Buildings?

An export project is not only about putting products into a container. Specifications, packaging, loading and the destination installation plan must work together.

CONTENTS01　Confirm the transport method first02　Protect and identify every package03　Review documents and local requirements

Confirm the transport method first

Flat-pack containers are often suited to flat packing or container transport. Complete modules require a review of unit dimensions, weight, route and port conditions before the method is fixed.

Protect and identify every package

Steel members, doors, windows, glass and electrical items need appropriate protection and fixing. Clear installation labels make sea transport and site checking much easier.

Review documents and local requirements

Packing lists, commercial invoices, product specifications and required certificates should be checked in advance. Local building, fire and electrical requirements should also be considered during design.
